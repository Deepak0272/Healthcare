Automated Insights

Intelligent Clinical Trend Analysis & Health Indexing

Health Tech integrates an automated clinical analytics engine that compares vital measurements against clinical reference ranges (AHA / ADA / WHO standards) and calculates statistical health trends over time.

  • Configurable Clinical Ranges: Systolic, diastolic, glucose, pulse, temperature thresholds.
  • Trend Direction Summaries: Mathematical delta and trajectory analysis.
  • Informational Warnings: Immediate alerts for abnormal readings without diagnosing.
Medical Disclaimer: Health alerts and trend calculations are informational and do not substitute for professional clinical medical advice or diagnosis.
